Severity colours now run red, orange, yellow, green, blue in both themes. `severity-medium` was a near-invisible grey and is now yellow (Light `#a65f00`, Dark `#ffdf20`). `severity-high` moves from amber to orange (Light `#f54900`, Dark `#ff8904`) so it stays distinct from medium. In Light, `severity-low` darkens to `#008236`, so its icon passes 3:1 contrast. No tokens were renamed or removed. `@vineethbangera/ui` is released too because its stylesheet includes the token values.
